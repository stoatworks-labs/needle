Photosensitivity warning

With Shutter above 0, the VFD flashes. Shutter (Type = VFD only) models a camera, and the VFD's multiplexed scan beats against the frame rate, so a dark band rolls through the display: measured at up to 30 flashes a second in part of the frame. That is far over the three a second that broadcast flash guidance (Ofcom, ITU-R BT.1702) allows. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Leave Shutter at 0 if flashing could affect you or anyone who can see your screen.

Measured from the plugin's own output (its test harness at 1280×720 and 60 fps, 15 s a run, on music from Resolume's demo clips), over the whole frame and in each third of it. With Shutter at 0, the default, the VFD's worst second had 1 flash at the default settings and 2 at its fastest ballistics. With Shutter above 0 it went over the line in a third of the frame (never over the whole frame): 10 to 30 a second from 1/60 s to 1/500 s, depending on Multiplex Rate, and 1 or under at 1/1000 s and 1/8000 s.

The page opens at Shutter 0. A link that opens on the VFD with Shutter above 0 opens paused, and a caution line under the picture repeats this whenever the VFD is shown. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
